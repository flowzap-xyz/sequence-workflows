# Conflict Resolution

Workplace conflict resolution workflow with complaint intake, HR investigation, mediation sessions, resolution documentation, and follow-up monitoring.

**Domain:** hr  
**Complexity:** 4/5  
**Tags:** [conflict-resolution, workplace-dispute, mediation, hr-investigation, employee-relations, grievance]

## Lanes
- **Employee** - Reports conflict, participates in resolution
- **HR** - Investigates, facilitates mediation
- **Management** - Supports resolution, implements changes

## Process Flow
1. Employee reports workplace conflict
2. HR conducts initial intake interview
3. Assess severity and determine approach
4. Interview all parties involved
5. Document findings and perspectives
6. Schedule mediation session if appropriate
7. Facilitate resolution discussion
8. Document agreed resolution
9. Monitor for compliance and follow-up
