# Offboarding

Employee offboarding workflow with exit interview, knowledge transfer, asset collection, access revocation, final pay processing, and benefits continuation.

**Domain:** hr  
**Complexity:** 4/5  
**Tags:** [employee-offboarding, exit-interview, access-revocation, asset-return, knowledge-transfer, final-paycheck]

## Lanes
- **Employee** - Completes exit tasks, returns assets
- **Manager** - Conducts exit interview, manages knowledge transfer
- **HR/IT** - Revokes access, processes final pay, handles benefits

## Process Flow
1. Initiate offboarding upon resignation/termination
2. Schedule exit interview with manager
3. Begin knowledge transfer to team members
4. Collect company assets (laptop, badge, keys)
5. IT revokes system access and email
6. Process final paycheck with accrued PTO
7. Provide COBRA and benefits continuation info
8. Conduct exit interview for feedback
9. Complete offboarding checklist
