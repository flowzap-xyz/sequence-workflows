# Termination Process

Employee termination workflow handling voluntary and involuntary separations with legal review, separation agreements, final pay calculation, COBRA notices, and severance processing.

**Domain:** hr  
**Complexity:** 4/5  
**Tags:** [employee-termination, offboarding, separation-agreement, final-pay, cobra, severance, legal-review]

## Lanes
- **Manager** - Initiates termination, documents issues, conducts final meeting
- **Human Resources** - Reviews request, handles legal routing, prepares agreements
- **Payroll** - Calculates final pay, PTO payout, COBRA, severance

## Process Flow
1. Manager initiates termination request
2. HR reviews and determines termination type
3. Involuntary: Verify cause documentation
4. Voluntary: Process resignation
5. Route to legal counsel if required
6. Prepare separation agreement
7. Calculate final pay and accrued PTO
8. Process final paycheck
9. Generate COBRA continuation notice
10. Issue W-2 instructions
11. Process severance if applicable
12. Manager conducts final meeting
