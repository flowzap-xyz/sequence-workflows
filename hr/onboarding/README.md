# Employee Onboarding

Complete employee onboarding workflow covering offer acceptance, HR record creation, benefits enrollment, background check verification, IT account provisioning, equipment setup, and first day orientation.

**Domain:** hr  
**Complexity:** 4/5  
**Tags:** [employee-onboarding, new-hire, hr-workflow, background-check, it-provisioning, benefits-enrollment]

## Lanes
- **New Hire** - Accepts offer, completes forms, attends orientation
- **Human Resources** - Creates records, enrolls benefits, verifies background
- **IT Department** - Provisions accounts, sets up equipment, grants access

## Process Flow
1. New hire accepts offer letter
2. HR creates employee record and enrolls in benefits
3. Assign to department and schedule orientation
4. Background check verification (rescind offer if failed)
5. New hire completes I-9 and W-4 forms
6. IT creates user accounts and provisions laptop
7. Set up email, Slack, and grant system access
8. Check equipment availability (order if needed)
9. Ship equipment to employee
10. New hire sets up direct deposit and completes orientation
