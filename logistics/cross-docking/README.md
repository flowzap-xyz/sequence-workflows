# Cross Docking

Cross-docking workflow with inbound scheduling, sortation by destination, outbound consolidation, and direct transfer without storage for just-in-time delivery.

**Domain:** logistics  
**Complexity:** 3/5  
**Tags:** [cross-docking, flow-through, dock-scheduling, sortation, consolidation, just-in-time]

## Lanes
- **Inbound Dock** - Receives and unloads shipments
- **Sortation** - Sorts by destination, stages for outbound
- **Outbound Dock** - Consolidates and loads for delivery

## Process Flow
1. Schedule inbound arrival at dock door
2. Unload inbound shipment
3. Scan and verify contents
4. Sort items by outbound destination
5. Move to outbound staging area
6. Consolidate with other items for same destination
7. Load onto outbound vehicle
8. Dispatch for delivery
9. Update tracking for all items
