# Delivery Confirmation

Delivery confirmation workflow with proof of delivery capture, signature collection, photo documentation, order status update, and customer notification.

**Domain:** logistics  
**Complexity:** 2/5  
**Tags:** [delivery-confirmation, pod, signature-capture, order-completion, customer-notification, tracking-update]

## Lanes
- **Driver/Carrier** - Delivers package, captures POD
- **Order System** - Updates status, notifies customer

## Process Flow
1. Arrive at delivery location
2. Verify recipient or safe location
3. Capture signature on mobile device
4. Take photo of delivered package
5. Record GPS coordinates and timestamp
6. Upload proof of delivery
7. Update order status to delivered
8. Send delivery confirmation to customer
9. Close delivery task
