# Reverse Logistics

Reverse logistics workflow with return authorization, product grading, refurbishment decision, recycling coordination, and secondary market disposition.

**Domain:** logistics  
**Complexity:** 4/5  
**Tags:** [reverse-logistics, product-returns, refurbishment, recycling, disposition, sustainability]

## Lanes
- **Returns Center** - Receives returns, grades condition
- **Refurbishment** - Repairs and reconditions products
- **Disposition** - Routes to resale, recycle, or dispose

## Process Flow
1. Receive returned product
2. Inspect and grade condition (A/B/C/D)
3. Determine disposition path
4. Grade A: Return to primary inventory
5. Grade B: Send to refurbishment
6. Refurbish and repackage
7. Grade C: Sell to secondary market
8. Grade D: Send to recycling
9. Track sustainability metrics
