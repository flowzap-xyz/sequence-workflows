# Subscription Lifecycle

SaaS subscription lifecycle workflow with trial signup, conversion nurturing, payment processing, renewal management, and churn prevention campaigns.

**Domain:** business-operations  
**Complexity:** 4/5  
**Tags:** [subscription-lifecycle, saas, trial-conversion, renewal, churn-prevention, stripe]

## Lanes
- **Customer** - Signs up, uses product, renews
- **Billing System** - Processes payments, manages subscriptions
- **Marketing Automation** - Sends lifecycle emails

## Process Flow
1. Customer starts free trial
2. Send onboarding email sequence
3. Track product engagement
4. Send trial expiration reminder
5. Convert to paid subscription
6. Process recurring payment
7. Send renewal reminder before expiry
8. Handle failed payment with dunning
9. Win-back campaign if churned
