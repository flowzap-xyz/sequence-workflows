# Cash Flow Forecast

Cash flow forecasting workflow with AR/AP projections, working capital analysis, liquidity gap identification, and short-term financing recommendations.

**Domain:** finance  
**Complexity:** 3/5  
**Tags:** [cash-flow-forecast, liquidity-planning, working-capital, ar-collections, ap-disbursements, treasury]

## Lanes
- **Treasury Analyst** - Gathers data, builds forecast, presents to management
- **Finance System** - Aggregates AR/AP data, calculates projections

## Process Flow
1. Gather accounts receivable aging data
2. Project expected collections by week
3. Gather accounts payable obligations
4. Project disbursements by due date
5. Calculate net cash position by period
6. Identify liquidity gaps or surpluses
7. Recommend short-term borrowing or investment
8. Present forecast to treasury management
