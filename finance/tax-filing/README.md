# Tax Filing

Corporate tax filing workflow with taxable income calculation, deductions, tax credits, variance review, and electronic or paper filing to IRS with receipt confirmation.

**Domain:** finance  
**Complexity:** 4/5  
**Tags:** [tax-filing, corporate-tax, tax-calculation, irs-efile, tax-credits, deductions, compliance]

## Lanes
- **Tax Team** - Gathers data, prepares schedules, submits filing
- **Tax Calculation** - Computes income, applies deductions and credits
- **Tax Review** - Reviews calculations, prepares documents, files return

## Process Flow
1. Tax team gathers financial data
2. Prepare tax schedules
3. Calculate taxable income
4. Apply deductions and compute tax liability
5. Check for applicable tax credits
6. Calculate final amount owed/refund
7. Review calculations for discrepancies
8. Prepare filing documents
9. Choose filing type (electronic or paper)
10. E-file with IRS or mail paper return
11. Confirm receipt of filing
