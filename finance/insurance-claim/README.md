# Insurance Claim

Insurance claim processing workflow with incident documentation, claim filing, adjuster assignment, damage assessment, and settlement negotiation.

**Domain:** finance  
**Complexity:** 3/5  
**Tags:** [insurance-claim, claim-filing, adjuster-review, settlement, property-damage, liability]

## Lanes
- **Claimant** - Reports incident, provides documentation
- **Insurance Company** - Assigns adjuster, processes settlement

## Process Flow
1. Report incident to insurance company
2. Document damage with photos and descriptions
3. File formal claim with policy number
4. Insurance assigns claims adjuster
5. Adjuster reviews documentation
6. Conduct damage assessment or inspection
7. Determine coverage and liability
8. Negotiate settlement amount
9. Issue payment or denial with explanation
