# Expense Claim

Employee expense reimbursement workflow with receipt submission, manager approval, finance review, policy compliance check, and direct deposit payment.

**Domain:** finance  
**Complexity:** 3/5  
**Tags:** [expense-reimbursement, employee-expenses, receipt-upload, manager-approval, finance-review, direct-deposit]

## Lanes
- **Employee** - Submits expense claim with receipts
- **Manager** - Reviews and approves employee expenses
- **Finance App** - Validates policy compliance, processes payment

## Process Flow
1. Employee submits expense claim with receipts
2. Manager receives and reviews expense request
3. Finance reviews expense details for policy compliance
4. Employee makes any required corrections
5. Manager updates records and approves
6. Finance processes approved expense
7. Payment issued via direct deposit
8. Employee receives reimbursement confirmation
