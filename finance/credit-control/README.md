# Credit Control

Customer credit control workflow with credit limit assessment, credit scoring, order hold management, collections escalation, and payment plan negotiation.

**Domain:** finance  
**Complexity:** 3/5  
**Tags:** [credit-control, credit-limit, customer-credit, credit-scoring, collections, accounts-receivable]

## Lanes
- **Sales** - Requests credit limit changes, manages customer relationships
- **Credit Team** - Assesses credit risk, sets limits, manages holds
- **Collections** - Escalates overdue accounts, negotiates payment plans

## Process Flow
1. New customer or credit limit change request
2. Pull credit report and assess credit score
3. Review payment history and financial statements
4. Set or adjust credit limit based on risk
5. Monitor accounts for overdue invoices
6. Place orders on hold if over limit
7. Escalate to collections if payment overdue
8. Negotiate payment plan if needed
