# Treasury Management

Corporate treasury management workflow with cash pooling, FX exposure hedging, short-term investment decisions, and bank relationship management.

**Domain:** finance  
**Complexity:** 3/5  
**Tags:** [treasury-management, cash-pooling, fx-hedging, investment-portfolio, bank-relationships, liquidity]

## Lanes
- **Treasury Manager** - Monitors positions, executes transactions
- **Banking System** - Aggregates balances, executes transfers

## Process Flow
1. Monitor daily cash positions across accounts
2. Execute cash pooling to concentrate funds
3. Identify FX exposures from international operations
4. Execute hedging transactions (forwards, options)
5. Evaluate short-term investment opportunities
6. Place excess cash in money market or T-bills
7. Manage bank relationships and credit facilities
8. Report treasury positions to CFO
